Journal history

Every honest line you have written is kept safely here on your device — one entry for each day. Look back and remember how your heart truly felt, the days that were heavy and the days you were grateful.

Your data — an amanah

Your data is an amanah — a sacred trust. Everything you write — your journal, reflections, duʿāʾ and searches — stays on your own device and is never uploaded. So the app can keep running well and you can recover your password, a minimal record is kept on our secure server: your username, email and anonymous activity counts (how many prayers you’ve logged, your streak) — never your writings. We never sell it, never share it, and never read what is yours. It remains yours alone, in shā’ Allah — kept the way an amanah deserves to be kept.
